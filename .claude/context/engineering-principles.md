# Engineering Principles

## Priority Order

1. Security
2. Maintainability
3. Simplicity
4. Reliability
5. Performance
6. Developer experience

## Rules

Prefer simple solutions.

Avoid premature abstraction.

Avoid unnecessary dependencies.

Do not over-engineer MVP features.

Make decisions that support future scale.

## When uncertain

Explain:

- options
- tradeoffs
- recommendation

Do not silently choose a complex approach.
