# SharePoint Knowledge Health

## Mission

Build an enterprise SaaS platform that measures and improves Microsoft 365 knowledge quality.

The platform helps organizations ensure their documents are:

- accurate
- current
- owned
- discoverable
- trustworthy

The product improves AI readiness for systems like Microsoft Copilot.

This is NOT a chatbot product.

---

## Current MVP

Feature:

Document Health Score

The system:

1. Connects to Microsoft 365
2. Retrieves SharePoint documents
3. Stores metadata
4. Analyzes document quality
5. Calculates health scores
6. Provides actionable recommendations

---

## Product Philosophy

Knowledge quality before AI.

A better AI system requires better source information.

Do not add AI features unless they provide measurable business value.
